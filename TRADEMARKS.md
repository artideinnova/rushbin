# The rushbin name and logo

The license of the code ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)) covers the code, not the name or the logo. "rushbin" and the rushbin
logo are Artide Innova SRL's.

You may, without asking:

- say that your site or product runs rushbin, uses rushbin, or is based on rushbin;
- run your own rushbin site under your own domain, for your teams, and call it rushbin inside your organization.

Ask us first (hello@artide.studio) to:

- distribute a changed version, a fork, or a hosted service under the name rushbin or with its logo: give it another
  name and another logo;
- use the name or the logo in a way that suggests Artide makes, endorses or supports what you do.
