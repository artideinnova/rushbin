# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in rushbin, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else on behalf of the project,
follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and criticize
ideas, never people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

Report a problem to hello@artide.studio, with "conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by Artide's maintainers,
kept confidential, and answered. Maintainers may remove comments and code, and ban people from the project, for
behavior that breaks this code.
